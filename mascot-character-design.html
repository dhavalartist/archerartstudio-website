THE PROJECT

A memorable mascot built from concept to 3D.

The goal was to create a friendly business-oriented mascot with a strong silhouette, readable facial features and enough personality to work across marketing, animation and interactive applications.

The final character combines a distinctive green shirt, red tie, blue trousers, glasses, moustache and document bag into a coherent visual identity that remains recognizable from multiple angles.

02 · CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

Idea › shape › final character.

DESIGN INTENT

Friendly enough for a brand.
