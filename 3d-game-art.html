OUR PROCESS

From First Concept to Final 3D Asset

Our workflow turns early ideas into polished, production-ready 3D artwork. Each stage is carefully developed to keep the visual direction clear and the final asset ready for production.

Establish the visual direction, proportions, style and overall design through references and initial concepts.

Build the character, prop or environment with clean shapes, useful proportions and production requirements in mind.

Add colors, materials and surface details that bring the model to life while maintaining visual consistency.

Prepare characters with suitable rigs and controls so they can be posed, animated and used in production.

Develop movement, poses and animation support required for the final game experience.

FAQ

3D Game Art FAQ

What types of 3D game art do you create?

Archer Art Studio creates 3D characters, props, game assets, environments, hard-surface models, textures, materials and rigging-support assets.

Can you create 3D characters from an existing concept?

Yes. We can develop a 3D character from sketches, concept art, references or an established visual direction.

Do you create production-ready game assets?

Yes. Assets can be developed with practical production needs such as visual consistency, materials, presentation and delivery requirements in mind.

Can you create both stylized and realistic 3D artwork?

We can support stylized and visually directed 3D artwork depending on the project’s references, art direction and requirements.

Can you support an existing game development team?

Yes. Archer Art Studio can support individual deliverables, project-based production and ongoing 3D art requirements.
